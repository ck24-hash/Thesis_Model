# Block 1: Buses and carriers (`add_buses`)

**Function**: `add_buses(n)` in [`blocks.py`](../../src/ghmodel/blocks.py)

## Purpose
Creates the nodes of the network. Every other block connects to these buses. A bus is a point where energy (or CO2)
must balance every hour. The names are the same in every scenario, so blocks can be reused.

## Components created
| Bus | Carrier | Represents | Unit of flows |
|---|---|---|---|
| `gas` | gas | natural gas delivery point of the site | MW (LHV) |
| `elec` | electricity | greenhouse electricity bus | MW |
| `heat` | heat | greenhouse heating network at supply temperature | MW |
| `tes` | heat | inside of the thermal storage tank | MW |
| `co2` | co2 | CO2 stream available for enrichment | t/h |

Carriers created: `gas`, `electricity`, `heat`, `co2`.

## Parameters
None.

## Inputs / outputs
None directly. Bus flows appear in the outputs of the blocks that are connected to them.

## Constraints tied to this block
**Hourly balance at each bus** (generated by PyPSA for every bus $b$ and hour $t$):

$$\sum \text{inflows}_{b,t} \;=\; \sum \text{outflows}_{b,t}$$

In the Baseline this gives:

| Bus | Balance (every hour) |
|---|---|
| `gas` | NG supply = boiler fuel use |
| `elec` | grid import = electricity demand |
| `heat` | boiler heat + storage discharge + unserved heat = heat demand + storage charge + heat dump |
| `tes` | charge into the store − discharge from the store = change of stored energy (see block 6) |
| `co2` | boiler CO2 + unserved CO2 = CO2 demand + CO2 vent |

In Scenario 1 the CHP and grid export join the same buses:

| Bus | Balance (every hour), S1 |
|---|---|
| `gas` | NG supply = CHP fuel + boiler fuel |
| `elec` | grid import + CHP electricity = electricity demand + grid export |
| `heat` | CHP heat + boiler heat + storage discharge + unserved heat = heat demand + storage charge + heat dump |
| `co2` | CHP CO2 + boiler CO2 + unserved CO2 = CO2 demand + CO2 vent |

`results.check_balances()` recomputes the `elec`, `heat` and `co2` balances from the results table (all terms, zero for
absent components) and stops if any hourly error is above 1e-5.

## Assumptions
- One heat bus at a single supply temperature: all heat sources are interchangeable (no temperature levels).
- The `tes` bus is internal to the storage, it has no other connection.

## Notes for S1 to S4
New buses are expected (for example low-temperature heat for the data-center heat source, `ethanol` for the SOFC).
They can be created inside the new blocks, but the bus list above and in `CLAUDE.md` should be extended.
